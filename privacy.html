Privacy & Cookies

Effective June 28, 2026

Images and model files

Files selected in the Image Pixels and 3D Models tools are processed in your browser. Pixel Perfect does not upload those local files to its own server. If you load an image from a web address, your browser requests that image from the address you provide, and that external site may receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

Local preferences

The site may store interface preferences, such as the selected color theme, in your browser so they remain available on later visits.

Advertising and cookies

When advertising is enabled, Pixel Perfect uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize, measure, and limit advertising. Ads may be selected using information about visits to this site and other sites.

You can manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings. Visitors may also use the privacy choices control shown in the site footer when Google’s consent tools are available.

Third-party services

Google’s advertising services may collect device, browser, approximate location, interaction, and advertising data under Google’s own policies. Review how Google uses information from partner sites for more information.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the site’s features, advertising setup, or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
